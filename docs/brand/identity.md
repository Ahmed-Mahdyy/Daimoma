# Daimoma — Brand Identity

Status: phase-001 deliverable (T004). Name approved by owner 2026-09-25 (D-001). Licensing/conflict findings recorded below; the owner elected to keep the name knowing them.

## Name

**Daimoma — ديمومة**

- **Meaning**: "continuity / permanence / things that keep running" (root د-و-م, shared with *dawam*, "work shift/working hours").
- **Rationale**: the product makes requests, approvals, and time tracking one uninterrupted flow — the workday keeps moving instead of stalling in scattered channels. The root ties it to working hours while the abstract meaning covers all modules. Not CDS-related; a real Arabic word as required.
- **Pronunciation**: day-MOO-ma. Risk: occasional misspelling "Daymooma" — addressed in `usage.md` spelling rules.

## Tagline options (owner picks; recommended first)

1. **"Your workday, uninterrupted."** — Recommended; states the continuity promise for both requests and time.
2. "Work, in continuity."
3. "Every request. Every hour. One flow."
4. Arabic: «عملك، بلا انقطاع» ("Your work, uninterrupted").

## Personality (3–5 attributes)

Clear, trustworthy, calm, supportive, efficient — an operations tool people rely on daily, not a novelty.

## Confusion & license check (actual findings, 2026-09-25)

- **Daimoma Express / Daimoma Lojistik** — Turkish shipping/logistics company (daimoma.com). Different industry; no HR-software overlap found, but the name is not unique.
- **ديمومة (Edama/Daimoma)** — Jordanian business-development & technology consultancy. Different industry.
- **Decision**: owner confirmed keeping Daimoma on 2026-09-25; risk accepted for an internal/competition product. **No trademark registration or legal clearance is claimed** — if this product is ever commercialized, run a formal trademark search first.
- **Assets**: logo/icon produced in `assets/brand/` are original SVG artwork created for this project. Fonts must be OFL-licensed (Inter for Latin; IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic) — verify download licenses before packaging.

## Logo concept (v3 prototype candidate — final approval deferred to React implementation, D-010)

Owner approved the Phase 001 design/prototype direction on 2026-09-25 but **did not approve this logo as final**; it will be reconsidered during React UI work (D-010, 002:T025). A simplified employee portrait with a small overlapping check-in badge: people first, with completed work represented by the check. Solid teal and mint shapes instead of gradients or hand-drawn strokes; the badge is legible without relying on color alone. The rejected v2 used interlocking arcs and a teal gradient; the owner requested a person/check-in direction instead. Wordmark is `Daimoma` (capital D) in Latin locales and ديمومة in Arabic locales — **one language per lockup, never both side by side**. Files: `logo-primary.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `logo-light.svg`, `logo-arabic.svg`, `icon.svg`. The SVG wordmarks depend on installed fonts and need a final typography check before production. Never mirror, stretch, recolor outside the palette, or place on low-contrast backgrounds.
